About the Creator

Hi, I'm Maks — welcome to the studio

Made by Maks Creative Studio started with two simple loves: a lifelong obsession with digital art — anime, comics, and fantastical worlds — and a genuine passion for making learning feel like play.

Every coloring book is hand-illustrated with the same energy I'd want on my own sketchbook page, and every workbook is built the way I wish my own school worksheets had looked: clear, colorful, and actually fun to sit down with.

Whether you're a parent, a teacher, or a big kid at heart who loves to color, thank you for being here. I hope these pages bring a little more creativity and curiosity into your home. 💙
